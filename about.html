Who I Am

I'm a little bit of everything. A dad, a husband, a veteran, and a scientist.

Growing up, my parents hated math. Anytime I asked for help with homework, I heard that it was too hard, or that there was no point learning it because we'd never use it. It's not like we're rocket scientists. I picked up that attitude and carried it for years. I failed precalculus twice. What finally changed was realizing it was never about how hard the material is. It's about learning how to learn.
